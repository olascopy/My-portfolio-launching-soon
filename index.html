Terms and Policy

Welcome to Konnekt

Welcome to Konnekt. Konnekt is a social app for students and young people to chat, share Moments, and post anonymously in Loops. By creating an account and using Konnekt, you agree to these Terms and Policy. Please read them carefully. If you do not agree with them, please do not use Konnekt.

How to Use Konnekt Correctly

Konnekt is built for honest conversation, friendship, support, and community. You must respect other users at all times. You must not harass, bully, threaten, insult, or harm anyone. You must not post spam, false information, hateful content, sexual content involving minors, or content that encourages violence or self-harm. Loops is anonymous, but anonymity does not give you permission to hurt others. If you post something harmful in Loops, your post can be downvoted, hidden, reported, and your account can be warned or banned.

Moments last for 24 hours and then they disappear. Loops posts are completely anonymous. No name, no profile picture, and no campus is shown to other users. Chats are private between you and the people you chat with. Voice calls are currently available in Konnekt. Video calls are not available yet. Stickers you create are 6 seconds long. You can add text and edit them the way you want. Images you receive in chats are saved automatically unless you turn this off in Settings.

Your Account

You must be a real person to use Konnekt. You must keep your password safe and you must never share your password, your verification code, or your one-time password with anyone, including people who claim to work for Konnekt. Konnekt will never ask you for your password. You are responsible for everything that happens on your account. You can change your email, password, phone number, and campus in Settings. Your phone number is only used to sync your contacts. It is never shown to other users.

Your campus determines which Campus Feed you see in Loops. You can skip choosing a campus during signup. If you skip it, you can add it back later in Settings, under Account, then Change Campus. You can only change your campus twice, and you can only change it every 30 days. This rule exists to keep Campus Feeds honest and local.

How People Can Find You

You control how people can find you. Your ID is private. If you turn on “With ID Only,” people must know your exact ID to find you. Your username is public. If you turn on “With Username Only,” anyone can search for your username and find you. If you turn either one off, people cannot find you with that method. If both are turned off, nobody can find you by searching. They can only chat with you if you start the chat or if they scan your QR code. You can change these settings at any time in Settings, under Account.

What We Collect From You

To make Konnekt work, we collect your email address, your username, your profile picture, your About text, your campus, and your phone number. Your phone number is only used to sync your contacts. Your campus is only used to show you the correct Campus Feed in Loops. It is 100 percent anonymous and it is never shown to other users. If you connect Spotify, we only see your current song and artist, which we show as your mood. We never see your full playlist, your listening history, or your private Spotify data. If you turn on Moments Read Receipts, other people can see that you viewed their Moment. If you turn it off, they will not know.

Privacy, Safety, and Reporting

Your safety matters to us. You can control who can see your Moments, your profile picture, and your About. You can choose Everyone, Contacts Only, or Nobody. You can control who can add you to groups. You can block and report any user, Moment, or Loops post that makes you feel unsafe. When you report something, our team reviews it. Do not share your location, your password, your verification code, or any private information with strangers. If someone makes you feel uncomfortable, block them and report them immediately.

Account Bans

If you break these rules, we may warn you, temporarily restrict your account, or permanently ban your account. Common reasons for a ban include spam, harassment, bullying, posting harmful or illegal content, creating fake accounts to abuse Loops, or trying to expose other users’ identities. If you believe your account was banned unfairly, you can appeal through the Help Center by contacting support. We will review your appeal fairly.

Deleting Your Account

If you delete your account, you will lose all of your messages, Moments, Loops posts, contacts, and everything connected to your account. This action cannot be undone. You can sign up again later with a new account if you wish, but your old data will not be restored.

Changes to These Terms

Konnekt may update these Terms and Policy at any time to reflect new features, new laws, or improvements to safety. When we make an important change, we will let you know inside the app. By continuing to use Konnekt after a change, you agree to the updated Terms.

About Konnekt

What Is Konnekt?

Konnekt is a social app built for real people, real conversations, and real communities. It brings together chats, Moments, and an anonymous feed called Loops, all in one simple and beautiful app. Konnekt was created for students and young people who want to talk freely, share their day, find support, make friends, and feel connected to their campus and to the wider world, without losing control of their privacy.

Loops - Speak Freely, Stay Anonymous

Loops is the anonymous heart of Konnekt. In Loops, you can post honestly without anyone knowing who you are. No name, no profile picture, and no campus is shown to other users. You can choose between the General Anonymous Feed, where everyone on Konnekt can see your post, and the Campus Anonymous Feed, which shows you posts only from people in your own campus. You can upvote posts you support, downvote posts you disagree with, comment anonymously, like comments, and add hashtags to help people find posts about a topic they care about. If a post receives three downvotes, it is hidden to protect the community, and the author can appeal it from the Loops page. Loops is a safe space for honesty, confession, advice, humour, and real campus conversations.

Chats - Connect With People You Know

Chats is where you connect directly with people. You can send messages, photos, videos, voice notes, and stickers. You can create groups, reply to a specific message by pressing and holding it, react to messages, delete messages, and select multiple messages to delete at once. You can make voice calls with the people you chat with. You can find people with their ID or with their username, and you control exactly how people can find you in Settings. You can create 6-second stickers with text, video, or images, and you can save your favourite stickers to use again. Images you receive are saved automatically unless you turn this off.

Moments - Share Your Day For 24 Hours

Moments are short posts that last for 24 hours and then disappear. You can post a photo, a video, or a text Moment with a colour of your choice. You can control who sees your Moments, and you can report and block from Moments. In Settings, you can turn Moments Read Receipts on or off. When it is on, people can see that you viewed their Moment. When it is off, they will not know. Moments are for quick, real, and temporary sharing.

Profile - Show Who You Are Through Mood And Music

Your profile is how other people see you. You can edit your profile picture, your username, and your About. You can share your thought in a small bubble above your profile picture. You can connect your mood to Spotify. When you are playing music, people will see the name of the song and the artist you are playing. When you stop playing, they will see the last song you played. If you have not played anything, they will see your mood thought. Your mood appears as a mood pill in chats and on your profile. You can preview your profile to see exactly how other people see it.

Built For Privacy, Safety, And Real People

Konnekt is built for real people. Your phone number is only used to sync contacts. Your campus is only used to show you the correct Campus Feed. Your Spotify connection only shows your current song. Everything else stays private unless you choose to share it. You can block, report, and control your privacy at any time. Konnekt is a community where everyone deserves respect, safety, and a voice.
