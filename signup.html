Terms and Conditions

Welcome to Konnekt

Welcome to Konnekt. Konnekt is a social app for students and young people to chat, share Moments, and post anonymously in Loops. By creating an account and using Konnekt, you agree to these Terms and Conditions. Please read them carefully. If you do not agree with them, please do not use Konnekt.

You Must Be 13+ To Use Konnekt

You must be 13 years or older to use Konnekt. By ticking the box in Welcome, you are saying: "I am 13 years or older and I agree to these Terms." If you are under 13, do not tick the box and do not use Konnekt. We do not ask for your date of birth separately. This rule inside Terms is your agreement.

How to Use Konnekt Correctly

Konnekt is built for honest conversation, friendship, support, and community. You must respect other users at all times. You must not harass, bully, threaten, insult, or harm anyone. You must not post spam, false information, hateful content, sexual content involving minors, or content that encourages violence or self-harm. Loops is anonymous, but anonymity does not give you permission to hurt others.

Moments last for 24 hours and then they disappear. Loops posts are completely anonymous. No name, no profile picture, and no campus is shown to other users. Chats are private between you and the people you chat with. Voice calls are currently available. Video calls are not available yet.

Your Account

You must be a real person to use Konnekt. You must keep your password safe and you must never share your password, your verification code, or your one-time password with anyone, including people who claim to work for Konnekt. Konnekt will never ask you for your password. You are responsible for everything that happens on your account.

How People Can Find You

You control how people can find you. Your ID is private. Your username is public. If both are turned off, nobody can find you by searching. They can only chat with you if you start the chat or if they scan your QR code.

Privacy, Safety, and Reporting

Your safety matters to us. You can block and report any user, Moment, or Loops post that makes you feel unsafe. Do not share your location, your password, your verification code, or any private information with strangers.

Account Bans

If you break these rules, we may warn you, temporarily restrict your account, or permanently ban your account. If you believe your account was banned unfairly, you can appeal through Help Center by contacting support.

Deleting Your Account

If you delete your account, you will lose all of your messages, Moments, Loops posts, contacts, and everything connected to your account. This action cannot be undone. You can sign up again later with same email as a brand new account, but your old data will not be restored.

Privacy Policy

What We Collect From You

To make Konnekt work, we collect your email address, your username, your profile picture, your About text, your campus, and your phone number. Your phone number is only used to sync your contacts. Your campus is only used to show you the correct Campus Feed in Loops. It is 100 percent anonymous and it is never shown to other users.

Spotify

If you connect Spotify, we only see your current song and artist, which we show as your mood. We never see your full playlist, your listening history, or your private Spotify data.

Your Choices

You can control who can see your Moments, your profile picture, and your About. You can choose Everyone, Contacts Only, or Nobody. You can control who can add you to groups. You can turn Moments Read Receipts on or off.

Your Safety

Never share your password, verification code, or location with strangers. Block and report anyone who makes you feel unsafe. Our team reviews reports.
